## 3. Does it survive costs? (taker strategy, rebalanced every h steps)

Half-spread is paid on every trade; fee scenarios in bps of notional. The headline taker fee is 5.0 bp.

| threshold (bps) | trades | hit rate | gross bps / period | break-even fee (bps) | net bps / period @ fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | 2,639 | 0.434 | 0.2652 | 1.024 | -0.6996 |
| 0.25 | 5,193 | 0.481 | 0.2313 | 0.851 | -0.7189 |
| 0.5 | 3,730 | 0.535 | 0.1359 | 0.608 | -0.5460 |
| 1.0 | 162 | 0.687 | 0.0046 | 0.366 | -0.0250 |

Verdict: the best variant breaks even at 1.02 bp, so as a taker strategy it **does NOT survive** a 5.0 bp fee. A forecast this short-lived is monetised by a market maker (see section 5), not by crossing the spread.
